### FAILED — correction A: the wall is 66, not 72, and "72" was never measured

Round 2 built an out-of-tree probe (path deps into the worktree), copied
`wide_f32_ops`/`m7dp_config`/`ladder_compile` verbatim, applied the ~20-line stage-3
retry, and swept n=55..80:

| | result |
|---|---|
| shipped tree | 57/58/59 → OK at 1968/2004/2040 B; **60 declines** |
| with the retry | **60–65 compile** at 1956/1992/2028/2064/2100/2136 B; **66 declines** |
| at n=66 | `vfp-wide-file+pool-grow(136)` → `#881: VFP spill slot offset 1024 exceeds the VSTR/VLDR [sp,#imm] range (1020)` |

**The wide file buys SIX locals, not twelve.** And the grow formula is not the
variable: byte-identical outcomes at the stage-2 size, at the `I64_SPILL_SLOTS_MAX`
cap of 120, and with the cap lifted to 400 — because ~128 eight-byte slots are the
addressable maximum under a 1020-byte offset and this family consumes about two per
local.

**Where 72 came from, and why this is the worse of the two failures.** It was in the
test docstring, never measured. Round 1 **promoted an untested docstring number into
the CHANGELOG headline** and into three artifacts, *replacing* `58 → 64` — where 64
was at least a genuinely compiling measured point. The correction made the number
less true. The *mechanism* round 1 named was right (the wall is the `[sp,#imm]`
ceiling); its subject did not exist.

**Second count: the correction did not propagate.** `RQ-78-WIDEFILE.yaml`'s
`verified-by` still said "the last compiling n was 58" — the exact sentence round 1
declared false — because the diff touched only the `description`.
