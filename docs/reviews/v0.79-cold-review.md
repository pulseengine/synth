Round 1 examined `187af4c1` — the release candidate as first pushed. Round 2
examined `a213b7f6`, the tree *after* round 1's corrections, because its brief is

nameable here without regress; the merge ritual's squash-fidelity line
(`FAITHFUL (diff_lines=0, head=…, merged=…)`) is the zero-diff tie between the

`**Release commit: \`…\`**`, which does NOT match — so step 7 silently took the
weaker hex-harvest route for a whole release. Measured here:

## How the rounds were run

Four reviewers in round 1 and three in round 2, each in its own detached git
worktree pinned to the commit under review, none of them with access to the live
release checkout. Dimensions: the release notes' claims; the eleven artifacts'
own evidence; the gate machinery; and — the third standing question, which is not
optional — whether any comment addressed to an external reporter violates the four
conduct rules.

## Round 1 — findings, and what they changed

Round 1 found false, unsupported or misleading statements across the notes, the
artifacts and two documents. Two of them were the operator's own, introduced
while fixing something else.

**1. The #1454 finding was itself true-about-the-wrong-subject.** It said a vacuous
close set leaves the guards "off" and firing "NOTHING". The vacuous state is
LOUD-RED: `check()` returns a `VACUOUS` failure and `main()` exits 1 either way.
The wording was true per-guard and false about the gate's verdict — the exact class
this release is named for, in the release's own headline machinery finding. What is
really lost is the per-issue INSTRUCTION: in the vacuous state, closing an issue
held open by a prior release produces no "reopen it" by name, only a remark about
artifact hygiene. Corrected in the notes AND in the published issue. The defect is
real and sharper than first stated: `scripts/tag_release.sh:94-95` runs the gate
behind `|| echo` and no CI job runs it on the real artifact tree at all.

**2. A false excuse was written into an artifact, and withdrawn.** It recorded that
the alias differentials' missing f32 leg was a CONSEQUENCE rather than an oversight,
because `(if (result f32))` does not compile so an f32 port would be entirely
`EXPECTED_SKIPS`. False: `scripts/gen_home_alias_class_1189.py` emits nine shapes
and exactly ONE is an `if`; the rest are three `select` forms, a function-level
`br_if`, a `br_if` into a value-carrying block, a `br_table`, a block fall-through
result and a loop. An f32 port has about eight EXECUTING legs, so the gap is real
and sits exactly where the reported defect lives. The excuse is withdrawn and the
harder reading recorded.

**3. The #1436 refutation has no committed evidence.** The notes asserted that every
f32 join shape synth accepts is bit-exact against wasmtime, naming six shapes. That
sweep ran in-session and nothing in the tree reproduces it: `grep -rl 1436
scripts/repro/ crates/*/tests/` is empty, and the shipped `join_alias_1189_*` and
`home_alias_class_1189_*` differentials carry i32/i64/promo legs and zero f32 ones.
The notes and the artifact now say so. Committing that oracle is a v0.80 obligation,
and under rule 4 landing it would still not be progress on #1436.

**4. Seven of eleven artifacts reported nothing at all** — no `landed:`, no
`disposition:`, no `verified-by:` — three of them `priority: must`. Each now carries
a disposition and a written outcome. The gate cannot catch this: R11's failure branch
(`status_evidence_check.py:1728`) requires `landed_txt` to be non-empty, so it fires
on "you recorded a landing but did not say why you are not claiming" and is silent on
"you recorded nothing", which is strictly less information. Its own error text cites
the v0.67 incident whose artifacts were silent in exactly this way. Filed as #1458
and re-verified by mutation after the fix.

**5. Four artifact titles had drifted from their bodies.** A title is pasted UNEDITED
into these notes by the rivet tool, so each was about to become the release's public
description of its own finding. PAGESIZE's stated only its premise and nothing of the
refutation that was its result; FLOATREACH's said one arm "misdescribes both" when it
is wrong only for the f64 variant; VARVE's stated the reporter's unconfirmed location
as settled fact; PINDEBT7's still carried a framing its own measurement had refuted.

**6. A documentation certification was wrong, and the lane certified it anyway.**
PINDEBT7 reported "CLAUDE.md is ACCURATE" having tested two of the three escapes
CLAUDE.md names. The third is false: a dict COMPREHENSION escapes neither the
tripwire regex nor `_pin_table` — `_count_dict_comp` exists precisely to read one —
and the counter-example is the LARGEST table in the population, contributing 39 of
the 84 entries. So the documentation told a reader the biggest table was invisible to
both layers. Corrected in `CLAUDE.md` and in the `claims.yaml` comment above the
tripwire, which carried the same error verbatim. What remains true is narrower:
`dict(...)` and `.update()` do escape both.

**7. "FOUR tables empty, all four from the original population" is arithmetically
impossible.** The original population has four members and one of them
(`selector_parity_197`'s `KNOWN`, 20 entries) is non-empty, so at most three empties
can come from it. The fourth entered in v0.66 via RQ-66-WATCHED. Stated at two sites;
corrected at both.

**8. An ordinal derivation was invalidated by the edit that wrote it.** ARCHMODEL
quoted `ls artifacts/release-v*/RQ-*ARCHMODEL*.yaml | wc -l` as returning 16. It
returns 17 at any commit containing the artifact's own file — in the one sentence
whose stated purpose is to be reproducible rather than remembered.

**9. "Proven potent in both new directions" was unsupported.** The window recognizer
is the release's only substantive instrument change, and no committed test or negative
control exists for either direction. Restated as the hand exercise it was, with the
gap named.

**10. The headline contradicted the recorded decision.** The maintainer promoted #1436
over PAGESIZE on 2026-10-01, on the ground that an external silent miscompile outranks
a capability gain. An earlier draft of the notes gave the slot to PAGESIZE. Corrected,
and the planning-PR sentence in LADDER that still said otherwise is marked superseded.

**Filed from round 1:** #1456 (the cold-review conformance gate passes on a record
naming a PREVIOUS release's commit — four shas tested, all DERIVED-PASS), #1457 (two
blind spots in the version pin sweep, both break-tested, both backstopped by `cargo

**Conduct — the third question.** No violation in the v0.79 window. 39 comments

## Round 2 — attacking round 1's corrections

Three reviewers at `a213b7f6`, briefed that this repo's record is that round 2
repeatedly finds MORE false statements INSIDE round 1's own fixes, and that a
correction can over-correct into a new false claim. It found **eight new defects
introduced by the corrections** and two pre-existing ones the rewrite had left
standing. The two-round discipline earned its place again.

One method limitation, stated because it bounds the classification: the whole
`## [0.79.0]` section is new in the release commit, so `git diff` cannot separate
correction-introduced from pre-existing. Every attribution below comes from the
commit message's own enumeration or from the brief, never from inference.

### New defects introduced by a correction

**N1 — a quoted command result that was not re-run after the sentence was narrowed.**
"`grep -rn 1450 artifacts/` is empty" — it is not; `RQ-79-JESSDIVERGE` mentions #1450
in prose. The substantive claim (no artifact names it in an `issue:` field) survives;
the quoted evidence did not.

This was the clause the commit message advertised as the improvement, which is exactly
where a reviewer should look.

**N3 — one inserted word inverted an issue's point.** The notes attributed "28 of 40
open PINNED issues were never revisited" to #1373. #1373 says "28 of 40 open issues
have not been touched since the day they were filed", and then says the opposite of
the gloss: "28 open issues have NO pin, so the method above says nothing about them".
The figure is also dated 2026-09-24 on the v0.72.0 tag, which the notes gave no as-of

**N4 — a third occurrence of a symbol the notes said appeared in one place.**
`__synth_mem_region` is also grepped for in `.github/workflows/spec-suite.yml:103`.

**N5 — two fixture families collapsed into one.** The `promo` leg belongs to

**N6 — the withdrawal over-corrected in one direction and under-counted in the
other.** "Nine shapes" is the count of control-flow JOIN shapes, not of what the

`br_table`'s index, both i32 by definition. Six or seven. The conclusion — that the
`EXPECTED_SKIPS` excuse was false — survives, and more strongly than the sentence
claimed.

**N7 — a correction conceded a point the cited issue denies.** Calling the arm's
message "plausibly accurate" on the chosen witness concedes too much: accuracy

int/float sequence", is falsified by the same module, which VALIDATES under
`--features all`; #1448's own heading is "The diagnostic names a cause the module does
not contain."

**N8 — a duplicated `and` at the seam where round 1 spliced a replacement sentence.**

**And one outside the tree: the PR body still carried the withdrawn claim.** #1455's
description said closing a held-open issue in the vacuous state "fires no guard at
all" and "the protection is absent" — the exact wording #1454 was corrected to retract,
and which the notes themselves call "true per-guard and false about the gate's
verdict". Provenance: body written 12:21:05Z, the issue corrected 12:43:01Z, and the
only later event was a force-push — so the prose was never revised. Corrected in place

### Round 2, third reviewer — the artifacts, and defects INSIDE round 2's own corrections

A third round-2 reviewer took the artifacts and found that several of round 1's
corrections were wrong, and that two of the corrections written DURING round 2 were
wrong as well. The pattern did not stop at one iteration.

**The headline finding: an emptied suppression table read as progress.** A correction
added, to `RQ-79-PINDEBT7`, "In every case the pins were closed by fixes" — about the
four empty pin tables. Three emptied because a fix closed the pins. The fourth did not.
`scripts/repro/home_alias_audit_corpus_1189.py:41-43` says so itself: the #1222
declared-width gate "removed the HIT, not the miscompile — `checkRange` still compares
`$to` at R1:R2 ... so #1226 stays OPEN and is simply no longer visible to this audit; no
line here can see it", and `:165-173` adds "the write that made #1226 visible is gone
while the wrong homing remains. A #1226 fix will not flip anything HERE." #1226 is OPEN
today. So one of the four empties means the defect became INVISIBLE to its oracle — the
opposite of debt being paid — and the sentence asserting otherwise was new text inside
the artifact whose entire subject is pin-debt honesty.

**A reason falsified by the commit that wrote it.** A correction added to
`RQ-79-JESSDIVERGE`: "Nothing in the gate notices, because this artifact has no
`landed:`." The same commit added `landed: "#1449"` to that artifact. This is the repo's
own "a number is invalidated by the edit that writes it", committed in the same change
that fixed another instance of it in `RQ-79-ARCHMODEL`. The conclusion survives — R11's
branch needs a `landed:` with NO `disposition:`, and this artifact has both — but the
stated reason was false.

**A correction that was still wrong after being corrected.** Round 1 found CLAUDE.md's
claim that a dict comprehension escapes both the tripwire and `_pin_table`, and deleted
"a comprehension" from the escape list. That left the PREMISE — "the tripwire sees only
a top-level `NAME = { ... }` dict LITERAL (matching what `_pin_table` itself can read)"
— which is the false half. And the replacement claim written in `claims.yaml` during
round 2 was also wrong: it said `.update()`-built tables have "no `= {` for the regex and
no `ast.Dict`/`ast.DictComp` for `_pin_table`, so those two shapes do escape both".
Measured on a throwaway probe: `KNOWN_UPD = {}` followed by `.update()` MATCHES the
regex and `_pin_table` reads it, returning **0 without raising** — a silent UNDERCOUNT
rather than an escape, and strictly worse, because the table sits inside the pinned
population, counts toward the `expect:` total, and contributes nothing. Only
`NAME = dict(...)` escapes as described. Both documents now say that, measured.

**Three more, each a claim stated more confidently than its evidence.** "All four CI
feature sets build" — ci.yml invokes at least seven distinct configurations and no
canonical four exists anywhere in the tree, while the pin's own comment demands "ALL";
since ORDEAL is the one artifact flipped to a claiming status, R2 actually reads that
field, and the done-when's "every CI feature set" is therefore not met in full. "#1447
declines at minimum pressure, so it is not a register-pressure defect" — synth's own
sub-message for that shape names the allocator and a spilled arm result, so the claim
had to be narrowed to what it can support: the shape is categorically unreachable rather
than pressure-dependent, which is not the same as the allocator being uninvolved. And
"120 exported functions" is 117: the extra three are one `(memory (export …))` per
module. `brtab` also turned out to have no f32 form at all rather than a skipped one,
because the home IS the `br_table` index.

**One open question recorded rather than answered.** Round 1's narrowing of the
`pop_float` claim rests on reading "`Reg` and `Spilled` are the integer variants" off
their doc comments. #1448 is precisely a case where f32 lowering is broken, so an f32
value sitting in a `Reg` entry would make the arm's message accurate by accident rather
than by construction — in which case the narrowing over-corrected. Settling it needs the
variant inspected at the decline, which was not done. The artifact now says so instead
of presenting the inference as settled.

Also corrected from this pass: a line-range citation off by one, a title that attributed
a reporter's localization while leaving the reporter's RATE unattributed next to this
lane's own finding that nothing flickers across 29 runs, and two artifact bodies that
still asserted what their own `verified-by:` retracted.

## What NEITHER round saw — recorded at the v0.80 cut, 2026-10-01

Round 2 examined `a213b7f6`. Exactly one commit landed after it and before the tag:
`f8809521`, the release commit. Eleven files moved in it, and this record is one of
them — all 340 lines of it were added by the same commit it does not cover. **A cold
review's record cannot review the commit that contains it.** That is structural, not an
oversight, and it is worth stating plainly rather than leaving for a later release to
discover.

Two things in that commit deserved a cold pass and did not get one:

**The only Rust change in the entire release.** `crates/synth-synthesis/src/liveness.rs`
lost three `&` borrows (`:5793`, `:9614`, `:9621`) to satisfy
`needless_borrows_for_generic_args`, a lint rustc 1.99.0 added. The change was verified
red-first against 1.99.0 (rc=101 with exactly those three errors before, rc=0 after) and
is line-count neutral, and `cargo test --workspace` was re-run unpiped afterwards —
3148 passed, 0 failed, across 168 suites. But no reviewer other than its author read the
diff. Zero Rust had changed in v0.79 until that point, so the release's cold review was
conducted on a tree with no code change in it at all, and the code change arrived
afterwards.

**The `#1436` updates.** `RQ-79-JESSDIVERGE.yaml` gained 67 lines in that commit,
recording the reporter's silicon confirmation and the arithmetic that eliminates `#1450`.
Those are claims about an external reporter's open defect, written after the last pass
that was looking for false statements.

**And one of the corrections in that commit is itself an instance of the next release's
theme.** `RQ-79-ORDEAL.yaml` was corrected there from `status: proposed` +
`disposition: partial` to `status: implemented` with the disposition removed. In its
pre-correction form — on `main` at `28196ec7` — its `verified-by:` prose opened with the
word `LANDED.` beside a non-claiming status, and every gate was green, because R11's
obligation is only that a disposition be *present*. Round 2 caught it by reading the
sentence. Nothing mechanical could have.

The remedy is not a fourth round; rounds have diminishing returns and round 3 already
found defects inside round 2's corrections. It is that the release commit is the one
commit no review covers, so what goes into it should be the narrowest possible set —
and when it is not, that should be written here, as it is now.

## What both rounds say about this release

Thirteen of the statements corrected across the two rounds were about the release's own
machinery rather than about the compiler, and two of the most instructive were
true-about-the-wrong-subject: the #1454 finding itself, and the "no CI job" clause
added while sharpening it. The theme — reach as a floor, cost as a ceiling, pinned
together — was not delivered; v0.79 pinned neither and accepted no input it previously
refused. What it did instead was find, in its own instruments and its own prose, nine
false or unsupported statements that no gate could see.
