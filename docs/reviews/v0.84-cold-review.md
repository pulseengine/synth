## COORDINATOR ADDENDUM — the potency verdict for `release_notes_from_rivet` DEPENDS ON UNTRACKED LOCAL STATE

Round 1 recorded that gate as **potent**: `--base HEAD` yields an empty `added`, so the
anti-vacuity guard fires with "Refusing to emit an empty section". The coordinator had
independently recorded it as **INERT**, with its own control: `--base HEAD` yields
`3201 added`, rc=0, and the guard stays silent.

Both readings were re-run side by side. Neither is wrong:

    review worktree (fresh)          .rivet/repos ABSENT     --base HEAD -> rc=1, guard FIRES
    coordinator checkout             .rivet/repos 535 MB     --base HEAD -> rc=0, 3201 added, silent

Same command, same commit, two environments. `added` is head-minus-base; the head resolves
`externals:` out of `.rivet/repos`, which is untracked and gitignored. Where that cache
exists, `added >= 3201` unconditionally and the guard CANNOT fire. Where it does not, the
externals are absent from both sides and `added` is genuinely empty, so it does.
