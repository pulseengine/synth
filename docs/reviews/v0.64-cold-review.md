### The severe one

I closed #1108 as "breaks the required Bazel build", attributing it to `z3-sys`
linking the system libz3, and made it the release's only evidence that the
dependabot hold had ever caught a breaking bump.

`crates/BUILD.bazel:167-170` states there is **no z3-sys in the Bazel graph**.
The real failure was a **504 Gateway Time-out** fetching `bazel-skylib`: Bazel
compiled nothing, the log contains **zero occurrences of "z3"**, and it was never
re-run. "Three siblings on the same base" was also wrong — their merge-bases
differed.

**Root cause, recorded because it generalizes:** the job logs were unretrievable
through the API, and rather than record *unattributable, needs a re-run*, the
release author reasoned from the job's **name** plus a plausible mechanism and
wrote it up as measurement. The existing "read the failure name, not the count"
rule extends to the failure's **content** — and when that is unavailable the
answer is "unknown", not an inference.
