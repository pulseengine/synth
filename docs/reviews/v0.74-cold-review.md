**This release's central thesis was tested against itself and held, twice —
and the instruments it repaired are still mostly blind.** 29 of 40 attacks got
through. The pattern is consistent and worth naming in one place:

> Every fix this release shipped closed the SPELLING the previous reviewer

That is not an argument for more gates. It is the measurement this release
exists to produce, and it is recorded here rather than fixed in the commit

## Repaired gates, each proven by mutation

**The silent skip (`islandpass_1331_execution_differential.py`).** A fixture
reverting from "compiles and executes bit-exact" to STATE-1 "refused" scored
**green**: STATE 1 returns 0, prints no `RESULT:` line, and the declared floor
counted subprocess INVOCATIONS, which a refusing compiler still makes.
Demonstrated with a wrapper disabling islands for `nested` only — rc=0, two
PASS lines, and the CI grep satisfied. The floor is now
`emulations >= 15` (3 fixtures × 5 args). Re-run under the same wrapper:
**rc=1, `VACUOUS — declared floor emulations >= 15, measured 10`**, while the
script itself still exits 0. That is why a floor counts work done, not calls
made.

**INV2 was documented and never implemented.** `island_invariants`' docstring
claimed "every LDR.W literal resolves to a relocated word". `reloc_offsets` is
built and never read — two lines, an assignment and an `.add`, no use. A gate's
docstring asserting a check it does not perform is this release's subject
pointed inward. Corrected to say so, and to name what partially covers the
intent (`execute()`'s `nrel >= 3`).

**Arm D (`1 * x`) had no coverage anywhere.** Reverting it to `is_dead`, AND
emitting the Copy from the constant instead of from `x`, both survived
`cargo test --workspace` (168 suites, 3144 passed) and the execution
differential. `test_algebraic_mul_one` covers only `x * 1`; the commutation was
never tested, so a silent wrong answer was free. `test_algebraic_one_mul` now
exists and **both mutations fail it**.

## The measurement that disagreed with itself

PINDEBT2 claimed "only 2 of the 5 arms are observably wrong". The reviewer
rebuilt the pre-fix compiler independently, ran its own five probes, and
measured **4 of 5**. Two measurements of one quantity disagreed — and **neither
was reproducible**, because both probe sets lived in scratchpads, in a
repository whose stated rule is "derive what you check against from the
artifact you ship".

The probes now ship: `scripts/repro/algebraic_identity_probes_1223.py`, wired,
executing each shape against wasmtime through the home-alias harness. Re-run on
a pre-fix build they give **2 of 5**; post-fix **0 of 5**. The reviewer's shapes
are not in the tree and their figure is not reproducible here.

**The correction is not the digit — it is the claim's scope.** Masking is a
property of the chosen probe, not of the arm. `x - 0` and `1 * x` are unsound by
the same construction whether or not a given shape catches them, which is why
all five were repaired and not only the two these shapes expose. "2 of 5" is
true of THESE FIVE SHAPES and nothing wider.

## Recorded, not fixed — each with its mutation

These are v0.75 candidates. Six gate changes in the commit before a tag is how
v0.72's second miscompile arrived.

- **STUBPROOF is defeated by replay.** A 30-line script copying three
  pre-recorded ELFs — right machine, right symbols, `.text` overwritten with
  `0xDE` filler — passes the whole step: rc=0, `CHECKS=12/12`, `RESULT: PASS`,

  levels, `./scripts/x.sh`, `.github/x.sh`, a python wrapper, a make wrapper, a
  heredoc, and `uses:`. One `uses:` mutation WAS caught, but only because that
  action's *name* contains "apt". The population is latent today: no required
